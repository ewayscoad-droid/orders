Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Order Tracking App ("the app") is a small internal tool used by our team to manage purchase requests. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

  • Your Google account email address. Used only to identify you, to record who submitted, approved, ordered, executed or received an item, and to decide whether you are allowed to perform a step.
  • One Google Sheet owned by our team. The app reads and writes request data (item codes, titles, quantities, prices, order numbers, dates, statuses and the email addresses above) in this sheet.
  • Sending email. The app sends reminder emails, containing a link to the app, to the people responsible for the next step of a request.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to operate the order workflow described above. It is stored only in our team's Google Sheet. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, we do not use it for advertising, and no person reads it except authorized team members. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Request records are kept in the Google Sheet for as long as the team needs them. You can ask us to delete or correct records that contain your email address by contacting us at the address below. You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps and services.
