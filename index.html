Order Tracking App

Order Tracking App is a small internal tool that helps our team manage the purchase and handling of marketing items (presents, gifts, content and transparency materials) from request to completion.

What the app does

  • Team members submit a request listing the items they need (code, title, quantity, price).
  • A designated approver approves or rejects each item.
  • A designated purchaser records the order number for approved items.
  • A designated executor records completion of the work, and a warehouse receiver records the return of items.
  • Each person who is responsible for the next step receives an email reminder with a link to the app.
  • The status of every item is updated automatically in a Google Sheet owned by our team.

How the app uses Google account data

The app reads only the email address of the signed-in Google account in order to identify the person and check whether they are allowed to perform a step. It reads and writes rows in one specific Google Sheet that belongs to our team, and it sends reminder emails to the people responsible for the next step. See our Privacy Policy for details.
